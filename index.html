One client · 2026 — now
Heat pump installer Mazowieckie, PL · ~170 pages · client name withheld

I joined a company with a generic WordPress theme, a homepage whose project gallery rendered as an empty grey box, and almost no search presence.

I rebuilt the structure and copy, fixed technical SEO and Core Web Vitals, launched an interactive map of completed installations, and split the blog into the four topics buyers actually search for.

Then I went after the structural problems: around sixteen groups of duplicate city pages competing for the same queries, consolidated with 301s; twenty‑seven dead URLs recovered instead of discarded; a relative link bug generating phantom pages.

Average position on non‑brand queries went from 27 to 13 over the quarter, impressions per day rose about 66%, and clicks roughly doubled. The most‑viewed page is a guide I wrote about government subsidies.
